My Flip 5 started showing signs of a crease on the inside screen at around the 1 yr mark. I took it to the official repairs and they replaced the screen cover and the issue showed up again in less than a week. I took it as a sign as my screen was damaged rather than the screen protector, and carried on using it as is for years. Until yesterday(August 2026).

I removed the old screen protector, wiped the screen real-well to remove years of accumulated dust and was surprised to see that the crease was only in the screen protector and the actual screen is immaculate.

Put in the new screen cover and sure enough the screen is flawless. Who would have thought!
