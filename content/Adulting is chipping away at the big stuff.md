A lot has been said about what adulting is and isn't. Some say it's balance, others responsibility, maybe even paperwork.

There is a common thread in all of these. I think adulting in it's most real form is just a lot of chipping away at a slab of stone, hoping that bit by bit what emerges is a life well lived. It is long, multi-step processes, that you work through bit by bit. And yes, often these bits do look like paperwork, or balancing between two sides of your life, and often even owning up to all the good and not so good you have done.
