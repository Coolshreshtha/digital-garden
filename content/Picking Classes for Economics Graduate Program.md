As I wrap up term 1 of my [[Graduate School]], I have to pick my classes for the upcoming terms, and since there isn't much guidance on it out on the internet, I figured I will outline my process.

I have previously outlined my reasoning for choosing an Economics Program for graduate school. An extension of that reasoning is around why I chose my *program* within the realm of Economics Grduate Edicatopn. 

I find it helpful to think of class selection in terms of the intended outcome for the program.
At the time of graduation, I would like to have skills in three categories
	- Technical Credibility 
		- Econometrics
		- How to model and translate processes 
		- Core theoretical ideas 
	- Domain Expertise
		- The specific area where I have a fair bit of knowledge 
	- Professional Narrative

Ideally, at the time of graduation, I would have a set of skills that signals that I can understand and learn further in a space.
