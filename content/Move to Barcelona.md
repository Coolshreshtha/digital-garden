I'm moving to Barcelona in a bit for [[Graduate School]], and like any international move, this process has a lot of moving pieces. I will outline my experience here.

By far the biggest step right now is getting a [[Spain Student Visa]]. Beyond this, key focus is finding housing, [[Learning Spanish]] and [Building Good Habits to Avoid Burnout]()

### Housing
 Reputable sites for looking for housing include
*  https://www.bcuhousing.cat/en : Personally the one I favoured the most, but my listing didnt have locks. Good cancellation policy and reliable
* https://www.flaternity.eu/ : Potentially what I will end up going with.
* Badi
* Spotahome
* Uniplaces 

I used housinganywhere.com but it included a 280 euro fee in very stict conditions, plus any housing agency fee.
