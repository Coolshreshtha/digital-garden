25th September 2025 is my 10,000th day on Earth. I have mixed feelings about this because the 20,000th wont be until February 10th, 2053. It scares me to think what that would look like and who would be around to celebrate it with me then (or maybe more so, who wouldn't). 

A lot has happened in this 10k, a big chunk of it beyond my memory. A better way to phrase this would be: Everything has happened in this 10k. My entire existence.

I want to celebrate this milestone but unsure how to. All the people nearest and dearest to me are not even in Spain. I'm at the [[Three Weeks]] mark in my grad program, and the social euphoria of meeting new people and possibilities is starting to settle down. But on the other hand, I'm getting a lot of joy in this country, and maybe even some groundedness from the fact that I'm here for at least a whole year, after a pretty interesting year so far where I've lived in 5 different countries. I liked that too, and I like now as well, just different seasons for different things.



I also feel like I don't take enough time out of my day to express gratitude for all that I have. Top of mind is my real, physical body. It has been with me for all of these 10,000 days, and then some. We've been through a lot together, and it has been a trusted companion (maybe also a bit annoying at times haha). I go into nitpicky tangents about different features of it all too often, but I also can't imagine living in a different one!


On another note, I can't believe I have done this waking up and going to sleep thing 10000 times, and still don't have a good process down for it. 


In the spirit of being a budding economist, I will make charts about the distribution of these days, and major milestones :D. There is a recency bias here for sure, because the recent stuff has been very formative.
