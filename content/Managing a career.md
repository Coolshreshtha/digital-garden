We've reached a level of complexity where managing is a whole job in itself. Think of product managers and agents for actors. The latter especially focus on managing their clients' career, from bookings to promotion, and what have you!

Even if you don't have an agent, I think managing your career is definitely a different set of skills than just being good at what you do. In my own experience with consulting, it wasn't enough to know your work. A lot of effort goes into making sure you get into the right meetings, the right projects, visibility with the right people, and even knowing the pulse of the market. I'm sure variants of this exist in all careers, and I wish to see more explicit discussion of it. It can be especially hard for someone new to the space without an extensive network,  to figure out these implicit processes, especially so in a more remote/hybrid world.
