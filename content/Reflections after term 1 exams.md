USING AI

There is also the issue of using [[The balance between using AI and getting value from Ai]]. I have talked about this before, but I'm still figuring out the right balance of using AI tools to aid my learning. 

For instance, during the recent exam prep, it was really helpful to have Notebook LM and use it to ask clarifying questions and explainations. 

On the other hand, I can definitely notice an illusion of competence creeping. For instance, in time crunches, I would often try a problem for a bit and then take help from AI, in a way that I thought was conducive to learning but isnt in reality. I think the better option going forward would be to take an L on the problem set rather than risking it in the exam worth 70%. Something to think about.
