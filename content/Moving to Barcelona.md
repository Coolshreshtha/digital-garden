By far the biggest step right now is getting a visa.

## Visa
As a student, national visa long duration.
### Visa
* Background Check 
* Health Insurance: 
* Medical Certificate: 
* Apostille: 

#### Background Check/ FBI record
Send a request ($18 ) and fingerprints (electronic or through mail). Once you get the document, send it for apostille yourself or with a service like Monument. [Monument](https://monumentvisa.com/fbi-apostille/)
Took 9 days for FBi to process my fingerprint card, but no notifications so I was anxious.  Exactly 2 weeks by Monument under rush service.

### Health Insurance
Sanitas expat, and atlantida. Need to disclose previous health issues and back and forth for a bit. Quote for student visa

### Medical Certificate
From any doctor that knows you well and includes the required statement. Then sent for apostille using IVS in Delhi

#### Diploma and Transcript
Request through the school and they can notarize it. Then they send it to the state for Apostille.


#### Appointment
BLS India has a whole portal where you enter your information and then book an appointment and pay for visa fees ~15oo inr.
