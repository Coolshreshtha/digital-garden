In a conversation with a friend (man) recently, he brought up how he thinks "women today prefer to identify as career women".

While I have no way of knowing how true this statement is across all women, I have an issue with the underlying assumptions here.

For one, I think the dichotomy between a "career" or "non-career"woman is a false one at best, and malevolent at worst. Most people prefer a path that gives them more of what they value from life. 

If anything, women are taking stock of the fact that the only thing that provides them the support and security necessary is being financially and other types of independent. This does not rule out inter-dependence through community and families, but the world in it's inequity (lower wages, by far a larger share of domestic labor even in developed countries). There is a lot of evidence that shows that traditiional institutions like marriages help the male partner far more than they help the woman in the relationship. 


We are also seeing a lot of insidious ideas pop up like the trad-wife and the lonely spinster cat lady. When I see them, I sense them as the panic of the classes who have historically benefitted from the status quo.
