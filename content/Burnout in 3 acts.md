 In what turned out to be a bit more than a year long sabbatical. 
The questions in this one started out as a response to [[Burnout Recovery Toolkit]] and then slowly evolved into a vsriety of questions.

## Act I: Warning signs
I find it hard to pin exactly when the burnout started to set in.  But I remember very vividly how I did not want to come back to Chicago after my winter 2023 break, if not for S.  Some more events happened over the first few weeks back in Chicago that made it abundantly clear that something serious was up. I turned to spirituality as well. Also Anhedonia. So I started to try and fix what I can and also look for a therapist.  I didnt find one until March but it was a much needed relief when I did. 

I distinctly remember crying a ton, sometimes because I was sad, but also sometimes because that was the only thing that made it through the numbness. Oh the numbness. 


Scary because I don't know what triggered the descent into numbness, or even when.

My job was boring and uncertain for sure, but I'm sure there was a lot more at play, so much so that my body decide a disconnect was better than facing everything. 

I remeber this as a very sad time, because everythign felt like it was hanging by a thread and I was trapped and there was a "dense fog" all around 



## Act II: tipping point
Regular weekly therapy started to make a differnce. 
At this point, the therapy and effort was starting to make a difference and things were looking up. I was already in a cadence, and trying to get into  role sthat were personally more meaningful at work. I was volunteering in Product Management teams, since that was an area I previously enjoyed and did a good job at, and also looked like that could lead me to more interesting things.

An then the shoe dropped. I got laid off right before I was about to usher in a near year on my birthday. 

Relief and fear went hand in hand. 

## Act III: rebuilding

This kind of started before the beginnign of act II. I remember I joined a singing class, A key goal of this phase was reconnecting with my self, whatever that meant. A lot of activites like stitching to dancing to anything else that made me even a tiny bit alive

A lot of structure and therapy. I used to have my weekly therapy session ritual, and slowly added more to it with time, such as a weekly schedule, then a weekly review date at venteaux starting september

WIth a ton of processing using DrK guide and my own individual therapy session and even DBT and RODBT. Education on working with my mind. 

Engaging in activities like camping, trains, but also interests, this is where I got into Economics. Worked with the ea guide 80k hours. 

<3 third spaces 


At the end of the year, I left Chicago. There was a lot that was still uncertain.
