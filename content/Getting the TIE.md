For my TIE card, I got an appointment in Sabadell. 

True intel that appointments go live Thursday at 3pm. Be ready with ideally more than one device and a Spanish number to get a confirmation code (I used a friend's.)

Went to the appointment with all the checklist docs, but was only asked for my EX-17 and tasa fee receipt, and form. They also asked for a photo and passport with the Spain Visa. 

The people at the police station were super nice, and were really happy when I said my sentence or two in spanish. It also made me really happy when they asked me for some things in spanish and it instantly made sense to my brain :))). 

Took some time to walk around Sabadell after, went to a consum to grab something to eat but they had no hot food section. So just grabbed a bag of puffs, and walked around for a bit looking for a place to eat. I did come across a couple of menu del dia places, pretty affordable ones at that. But i kind of chickened out at the prospect of having to figure out what to eat and ask for it from the buffet style offering. The number of decisions was kinda high for my hungry brain so I skipped it and took the train back to UAB. Once back, I got a cafe and a really sad bikini sandwich which was just bologne and cheese in sad toasted bread. Not worth 3.95 but hey I tried a new thing.
