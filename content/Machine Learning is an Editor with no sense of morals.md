In a recent Cal Newport podcast, he goes a lot into the two tower architecture that curates FYP on tiktok and similar recommendation based news-feeds. But then he puts on his ethicist hat and talks about the issue here. See machine learning systems are created to prioritise a goal and go relentlessly towards it (in this case creating recommendations that keep you for on the app for the longest time). Since this is a values agnostic system, it literally has no qualms about tapping into the deep, dark side of human impulses. He mentions that the last time we let the darkest human impulses lose, we had the early 20th century chaos. 

As an extension, build your information systems, and for young people, your moral systems, around human curation and human intervention. Make sure there is a value-based human filter as much as possible. Think magazines and newspaper editors. 

He also mentioned an interesting area of Digital Ethicist. We cannot make decisions well about how to use a specific tech if we dont know how it works at the core.
