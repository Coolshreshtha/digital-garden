tl;dr : Your digital garden is your little corner of the internet that is personalized, not algorithm driven, and intentional. It is also semi-public, so makes for a great playground to learn in public, and share your process with the world.

Unlike social media, the goal here is not to curate your best side, but to share the imperfect process of being you. Since the format is largely text driven (though there are very cool non-textual examples out there too!), the focus is not to put out polished pieces, but instead to showcase the iterative process of forming an idea (and even abandoning some, and pruning others)
