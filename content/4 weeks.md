Today marks the end of week 4 of [[Graduate School]]. There's this peculiar thing about time that it manages to feel too long and too short at the same time.

I've been in Barcelona for a little less than a month now. 

Beautiful city, really like the energy but also so close to nature

FInally settling into a pace, with school and packing lunch (twice in a week)

Cool to learn about myself as well. I feel like i'm seeing a new side of me that i hadnt seen in a while. The last few years are clearly showing as i see bits of them reflect in me. 

BSE has been pretty fun so far. The program started off really intense but has now settled into a busy but not painful pace (I hope I don't jinx it). We are also doing stuff that feels like proper economics and that has been a bit of a learning curve but in a very fulfilling way.
